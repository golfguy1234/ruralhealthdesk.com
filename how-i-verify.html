How I verify

These are the working rules of the registry. They are not aspirations; they are what the build scripts and the review passes actually enforce.

  • Primary document or no verdict. I do not record a program fact or an award from an announcement or a news roundup. I read the governing document itself.
  • A number without a source does not get written down.
  • Verbatim text stays verbatim. When a state’s sentence describes an award, the registry stores that sentence unchanged.
  • Absence claims name where I looked. “I could not find it” is a complete sentence, and it appears in my notes.
  • Sources are archived with dates, so a page that later changes or disappears does not take the evidence with it.

How errors get caught

Nothing is called done on my own say-so. Every build gets an independent adversarial pass, and the registry is measured against the states’ own published rosters rather than against my opinion of it. What this does not cover

  • Published dollars only. Some executed awards have no published amount. They are counted as awards and never added to the dollar total.
  • Whether a specific vendor is inside an organization’s award is often not observable from public documents. Where it is not, the registry says unknown instead of guessing.
  • When two organizations’ names look alike but the documents do not prove they are the same, they stay unlinked.
