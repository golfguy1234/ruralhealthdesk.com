The service

Rural Health Desk

You get support identifying opportunities and building relationships with rural hospitals, clinics and other healthcare organizations.

Business development & outreach

Research target organizations, contact decision-makers and manage follow-up through meetings and commercial proposals.

Grant strategy & applications

Assess funding opportunities, develop partnerships and prepare complete applications. Grant work focuses on the CMS Rural Health Transformation Program.

Engage Rural Health Desk for either service or both, alongside your team and existing partners.

The work starts with your offering, target markets and existing relationships. You agree on the scope and retain control of commercial decisions. An engagement can include:

01

Find relevant organizations

Research organizations, their current providers and purchasing needs. Where grants are involved, check the project’s eligibility and funding requirements.

02

Reach decision-makers

Find the right contacts, write and conduct agreed outreach, handle follow-up and arrange meetings with interested organizations.

03

Prepare for every meeting

Get a brief on the organization and attendees, with talking points, questions to resolve and any relevant funding or purchasing requirements.

04

Write proposals and applications

Develop commercial proposals or complete grant applications with participating organizations. Your team approves its commitments; the applicant approves and submits the application.

Follow the work

A shared CRM shows contacts, outreach, responses and next steps, with owners and dates.

The research system

Rural Health Atlas

Rural Health Atlas is the research system behind Rural Health Desk. It brings together healthcare organizations, published funding awards and project details.

Automated monitoring and AI-assisted document research help identify opportunities. Findings are checked against the original sources.

Rural Health Desk uses this evidence to assess which projects may fit your offering, then investigates purchasing needs and whether outside support is still needed.

About Justin

I built the Atlas because I needed the answer, too.

I believe rural Americans deserve better access to quality healthcare. I help connect rural health organizations with companies that offer the technology and services they need.

I started this work while helping a health-tech company figure out where it fit in the Rural Health Transformation Program. It was confusing. The information was spread across state plans, award documents, spreadsheets and procurement pages, and I never found one place that had all of it.

So I read them, and I compiled everything I found into one database. That database became Rural Health Atlas, and the whole thing has turned into my full-time job.

That work has grown beyond grants. I now help healthcare companies pursue broader opportunities in rural healthcare.
